You may use the Bibalak word mark without prior written permission in the following truthful, non-confusing ways:

1. **Talking about Bibalak:** Referring to Bibalak in articles, blog posts, tutorials, videos, academic research, or community discussions, provided you do not imply official endorsement or sponsorship.
2. **Describing compatibility:** Stating truthfully that your plugin, deck pack, or companion tool is `"for Bibalak"` or `"compatible with Bibalak"` (as long as Bibalak is not part of your product's own brand name).
3. **Unmodified official builds:** Redistributing exact, unmodified source releases or official installers/binaries published by Hassanein Hassan Alkahafji, with all original notices, signatures, and attribution intact.

## 4. How Forks Must Rename

Because Bibalak is `AGPL-3.0-or-later`, you are welcome to fork the code and publish your own version—provided you **rebrand it clearly** so users cannot mistake your fork for an official Bibalak release:

1. **Choose a distinct name and icon:** Pick a product name and application icon/logo that are clearly different from Bibalak's marks.
2. **Replace branding in the app and installers:**
   - Update UI window titles, About dialog, product name, and installer/bundle metadata.
   - Change the application bundle identifier (in Tauri configuration) to your own reverse-domain ID so your fork does not collide with official Bibalak installations or user data folders.
   - Disable or point the in-app updater manifest URL to your own release infrastructure.
3. **Add a fork notice:** State clearly in your `README.md`, website, and About screen that your project is an independent fork based on Bibalak, and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Hassanein Hassan Alkahafji.
4. **Preserve legal notices:** Keep `LICENSE` (`AGPL-3.0-or-later`), `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md` (including the Synapse MIT notice), and copyright notices intact as required by the licence.
